Aether Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Aether uses account information, submitted profile details, developer applications, game metadata, and uploaded game/source archives to operate the service and moderate submissions. Do not upload passwords, secrets, identity documents, or unrelated personal information.

The starter version uses self-declared age fields only; it does not verify identity or age. Do not represent self-declaration or AI review as verified. Configure retention, deletion, access, incident response, and support processes before launch.

Supabase hosts authentication, database, and private submission storage. Configure project access controls and review the provider's privacy terms. This starter notice is not legal advice and must be reviewed by a qualified adult and lawyer before public launch.
