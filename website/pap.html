Privacy and Policy

This Privacy and Policy page explains how information is used when you visit the Aether website, create an account, apply for developer access, submit a game, or use Aether's catalog and related services (“Service”). It should be read together with the Aether Terms of Service.

Aether uses the information described below to provide accounts, operate the platform, review developer applications and game submissions, maintain security, and make approved game listings available. Do not submit passwords, payment details, identity documents, or unrelated sensitive personal information through Aether forms.

1. Information Aether handles

  • Account information: email address and account identifiers used by the authentication service, plus account creation and sign-in state.
  • Age and eligibility information: date of birth and age confirmation supplied during account registration, and date of birth supplied with a developer application.
  • Developer application information: developer or studio display name, portfolio and project information, application status, review status, and related account identifier.
  • Developer account information: role (such as player, developer, or administrator) and an Aether developer public key generated for approved developer accounts.
  • Game submission information: game title, description, executable and package paths, uploaded game files, manifest information, submission status, and moderation review information.
  • Library information: records of games added to an account's Aether library.
  • Technical and operational information: information processed by the website, authentication provider, hosting provider, and storage services as needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the Service. The exact logs and retention periods depend on the settings of those providers.

2. How information is used

Aether uses the information above to:

  • Create and authenticate accounts, including handling email confirmation.
  • Apply the minimum age rules for accounts and developer applications.
  • Display account features and maintain a user's library.
  • Review developer applications and determine developer eligibility.
  • Receive, store, review, approve, reject, and distribute game submissions.
  • Operate the store, developer tools, and moderation queue.
  • Protect accounts and the platform, investigate abuse, enforce the Terms of Service, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Age information

Aether accounts are restricted to people aged 13 or older. Developer applications and publishing privileges are restricted to people aged 18 or older. The current website collects self-declared dates of birth and age confirmations; this does not constitute verified identity or age assurance. Do not falsify age information or submit another person's date of birth.

4. Game files and public information

Game packages submitted for review are stored in private submission storage and are intended to be accessible to the submitting developer and authorized moderators. Aether may make files for an approved game available through public game-distribution storage so that the Windows app can deliver that game. Approved game listings may display information such as the game title, description, and cover image. Do not include personal information, credentials, secrets, or private data inside a game package or its metadata.

Developer public keys are identifiers intended for use in developer integrations and package manifests. They are not passwords. Never place a Supabase secret key, service-role key, access token, or other credential in a public manifest or game package.

5. Service providers

Aether uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and file storage. Those services process information on Aether's behalf to provide the platform. Their handling of information is also subject to their own terms and privacy documentation. Aether may use website hosting and content-delivery services to make the website available. Provider infrastructure and processing locations may vary.

6. When information may be shared

Aether does not make account email addresses, dates of birth, or private developer application details public as part of ordinary game listings. Information may be disclosed:

  • To service providers that host or operate parts of Aether.
  • To authorized moderators who need it to review applications, submissions, reports, or platform safety issues.
  • When necessary to comply with law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce the Terms, investigate suspected abuse, or protect Aether and its systems.
  • As part of a business transfer or restructuring, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate accounts and the Service, complete moderation, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Different information may have different retention periods. Backups and provider logs may persist for a limited period after information is changed or removed. Aether's current website does not describe a self-service account deletion workflow; do not assume that deleting an app or signing out deletes account data or uploaded files.

8. Security

Aether uses access controls and private storage for pending game submissions. No online service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a unique password, protect access to your email account, sign out on shared devices, and never share authentication tokens or secret keys. Report suspected account or platform security problems through the contact method made available by the Aether operator.

9. Children's privacy

Aether is not intended for children under 13 and does not permit them to create accounts. If the operator learns that an account belongs to someone under 13, the account and related information may be restricted or handled as required by applicable law. Aether does not intentionally request identity documents through its current website forms.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict some processing. These rights are subject to applicable law and exceptions. Use the contact method made available by the Aether operator to make a privacy request. Aether may need to verify that the request relates to your account before acting.

11. Cookies, local storage, and authentication

The website uses the Supabase client to manage authentication and session state in the browser. Your browser may store session-related information so you can remain signed in. Browser settings can clear or restrict local storage, but doing so may sign you out. The website's hosting and service providers may also use technical storage or logs required to deliver and protect their services.

12. Changes to this policy

Aether may update this page as the Service, its providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated date will be shown at the top of the page. Continued use of Aether after an update means the revised policy applies to subsequent use, to the extent permitted by law.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method made available by the Aether website or its operator. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, or secret keys in a privacy request.
